# BIS Roles and Terms

**BIS (Bicol Information Scrutineering System)** runs the results of dancesport competitions in the Bicol region. This guide explains who does what at a competition, what each person does in BIS, and the terms used on result sheets.

## Who does what

| Role | Who they are | What they do at the competition | What they do in BIS | Device |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Admin** | The organization's system owner | Manages the system between competitions | Creates competitions, staff accounts and adjudicator panels; sets the points table; checks and approves entry payments | Laptop (online) |
| **Secretariat** | Registration and event staff | Handles entries, back numbers, schedule and late changes | Sets up events, enters or imports competitors, assigns back numbers, marks scratches and on-site payments, prints heat and recall lists | Laptop 1 |
| **Scrutineer** | The person responsible for results (the "Executor" on result sheets) | Runs every round, checks marks, calculates results | Starts rounds and heats, sets recall numbers, watches adjudicators submit in real time, resolves ties, approves and publishes results | Laptop 2 |
| **Chairperson of Adjudicators** | The senior adjudicator who supervises the panel | Oversees fairness, decides ties at the recall cutoff with the scrutineer, signs results | Views live submissions and the calculation, confirms tie decisions | Laptop 2 or 3 |
| **Adjudicators** | The adjudicators on the panel, usually 7 and up to 10, each with a letter (A, B, C…) | Watch the dancing and mark it | Sign in with a PIN, mark an X for each competitor to recall in early rounds, place finalists 1st to 6th or 7th in the final | Phone, one each |
| **MC / Announcer** | The host on the microphone | Calls competitors to the floor and announces results | Reads the next announcement (recalls, finalists, results from last place up) | Laptop 3 or venue screen |
| **Competitors** | Couples or solo dancers, each with a back number | Dance in their events | No login at the venue; see results, history and rankings on the public website | Their own phone |
| **Clubs / Coaches** | Dance clubs, studios or teams (the "Country" column on sheets) | Enter their dancers and pay entry fees | Register dancers to events online, upload proof of payment | Phone or laptop (online) |
| **Public** | Parents, fans, other clubs | Watch | Browse results, past competitions and rankings, updated live at the venue | Phone, on the venue guest Wi-Fi or the internet |

## Devices at a typical competition

| Device | Used by | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Laptop 1 | Secretariat | Entries, schedule, printing heat and recall lists |
| Laptop 2 | Scrutineer (and chairperson) | Main BIS server, runs rounds and results |
| Laptop 3 | MC or venue screen | Announcements and live results display; also a backup if Laptop 2 fails |
| 10 phones | Adjudicators | Marking each round |
| Router | Everyone | Venue Wi-Fi that works without internet: a password-protected staff network, plus an optional guest network where spectators can only view results and rankings |

## Terms on result sheets

| Term | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Event number | Each event in the program gets a number | **E47** = Event 47 |
| Back number | The number pinned to the competitor's back | 60, 107, 179 |
| Grade | Age group plus class plus dance | JUN 1 - E1 (Cha Cha Cha) |
| Age group | Category by age | Juvenile, Junior I, Junior II, Youth, Adult, Senior |
| Class | Skill level of the event | E1, D, C, B, A |
| Discipline | Style of dancing | Latin or Standard |
| Solo / Couple | Dancing alone or as a pair | "6 Solos (Total: 21 Solos)" |
| Round 1, Round 2, Quarter-final, Semi-final, Final | Rounds that cut the field down, for example 30+ → 24 → 18 → 12 → 6 | |
| Heat | A group dancing at the same time when a round is too big for the floor | Round 1 in 3 heats of 10 |
| Recall / Callback | Being chosen to go to the next round | "Qualified Solos for the final" |
| X mark | An adjudicator's vote to recall a competitor, used in every round before the final | |
| Placing | An adjudicator's ranking in the final, 1 is best | 1st to 6th or 7th |
| Skating System | The official method, used under WDSF rules, that turns adjudicators' placings into final results | |
| Majority | More than half of the adjudicators; 4 of 7, or 6 of 10 | |
| Ranking points | Points earned per placing, added up across competitions in a category | |

## Points table

The client does not have a points table yet. BIS includes one the admin can edit at any time. This is the suggested starting table for the client to approve:

| Result | Points |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | 10 |
| 2nd | 8 |
| 3rd | 6 |
| 4th | 5 |
| 5th | 4 |
| 6th | 3 |
| 7th (7-competitor final) | 2 |
| Semi-final | 1 |

Rankings are kept per category, for example Junior I E1 Latin, and include every BIS competition in the season. At the venue, rankings update the moment the scrutineer approves a result, even with no internet; the public website catches up when the venue's results upload.
