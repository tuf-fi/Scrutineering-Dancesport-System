## The entry forms

All three forms start with the same block: province, team, coach, and for each dancer name, date of birth, address, email and contact number.

**Single dance events** list ten boxes, one per dance, and the dancer ticks the dances they enter. Each ticked dance is its own event:

| Latin | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| S Samba, C Cha Cha Cha, R Rumba, PD Paso Doble, J Jive | W Waltz, T Tango, VW Viennese Waltz, F Foxtrot, Q Quickstep |

The boxes are laid out as `S C R W T VW` then `PD J F Q`. The BGB Juvenile 1 and 2 solo events use the same ten dances in another order, `S C J W T Q` then `PD R F VW`: only the box order differs.

**Grade C events** (names ending in C) have a Latin set and a Standard set: Juvenile `S,C,J` and `W,T,Q`; Junior, Youth and the school events `C,R,J` and `W,T,Q` (Inter-Elementary uses `S,C,J`).

| Age group | Born | Ages in 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Juvenile 1 | 2017 or later | 9 and below |
| Juvenile 2 | 2015 or 2016 | 10-11 |
| Junior 1 | 2013-2014 | 12-13 |
| Junior 2 | 2011-2012 | 14-15 |
| Youth | 2008-2010 | 16-18 |

**Solo form:** Solo Juvenile 1, Juvenile 2, Junior 1, Junior 2, Youth, each as single dances and Grade C; then BGB Solo of the same groups.

**Couples form:** Couples events Juvenile 1 to Youth (single dances and Grade C), Inter-Elementary C, Inter-High School C, Inter-Collegiate C (photocopy of proof of enrollment); BGB couples events of the same list; then Ranking events (BGR): Juvenile C (born 2015 or later), Junior C (born 2011-2014, 12-15), Solo Junior C Female, Youth C (2008-2010), Solo Youth C Female.

**Closed Camarines Sur form:** the same solo and couples events with a CCS prefix (CCS Solo Juvenile 1 to Youth, CCS couples Juvenile 1 to Youth and the three school events).

## Rules and guidelines (the same on every form)

1. Entry forms and documents by email to the tournament address by **November 9, 2026**. New competitors and members add a 2x2 photo, a membership form, a birth certificate copy, proof of enrollment (Inter-Collegiate) and the entry fee receipt. Registered ones send only the entry form.
2. Entry fee due by **November 20, 2026**; failure to pay is subject to disqualification of the entry.
3. Fees: Solo Juvenile 400, each additional entry 200. Solo Junior and Youth 600, additional 200. Couples Juvenile and Inter-Elementary 700, additional 200. Couples Junior, Youth and Inter-High School 850, additional 250. Inter-Collegiate and all Ranking events 1000.
4. Membership 500, valid until December 2026. Athletes 500, coaches 1000.
5. Coaches attend the Bicol Coaches Summit, fee 1000 (listed twice, rules 6 and 7).
6. Fees are non-refundable and non-transferable. Always wait for the event secretariat's team summary before paying.
7. BGB (Bicol Grassroots Beginners) is for athletes without regional or national experience. BGB athletes may not enter solo, couples or ranking events. The top 3 BGB medalists may not enter the same event at the next Grassroots tournament.
8. Age is by year of birth.
9. Juvenile dress code follows WDSF; Junior events may use open costumes; Juveniles 1 and 2 follow the PDSF/WDSF syllabus.
10. Recipients of the Grassroots benefits: couples must enter all grassroots competitions of the quarter; points at the end of the quarter decide; a withdrawing recipient's benefit passes to 2nd or 3rd.
11. The organizer may change or cancel events and the program. The chairperson's and adjudicators' decision is final.
12. Payment is by GCash or Maya (the account is on rule 21 of each form; enter it under Fees and payment details).
