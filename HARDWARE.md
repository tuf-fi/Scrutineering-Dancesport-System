## Why these phones are enough

| Need | Redmi A5 (4GB/128GB) | POCO C71 | TECNO Spark Go 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Android 15 with current Chrome | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Screen | 6.88 inch, 120Hz | 6.88 inch, 120Hz | 6.67 inch, 120Hz |
| Battery | 5,200mAh | 5,200mAh | 5,000mAh |
| Memory and storage | 4GB / 128GB | 4GB / 64GB, or 6GB / 128GB | 4GB / 64GB |
| Chipset | Unisoc T7250 | Not confirmed | Unisoc T7250 |

- **Redmi A5 (recommended):** The biggest screen with the most storage at its price. 128GB leaves plenty of room for the offline copy and any unsent marks. The large screen suits the 56px touch targets, and the 5,200mAh battery should last a full competition day with the screen on.
- **POCO C71:** Same screen and battery as the Redmi A5. Check which version you are buying: the 64GB model is enough for BIS, but 128GB gives more room. I could not confirm its chipset, so test one before buying all ten.
- **TECNO Spark Go 2:** The smallest screen and battery of the three, but it still runs a web page like this. It is the choice if the budget has to stretch to spares.

### What could still go wrong

- **Slow chipset:** All three are entry-level. A page should load and respond fine, but a very slow phone could lag when the marking list is long (a final with many couples). Test with the biggest event before buying the rest.
- **Chrome updates:** Install Chrome and update it before the event. Turn off automatic updates on the day so nothing restarts mid-round.
- **Battery over a long day:** Charge to full the night before. Turn the screen brightness down and close other apps.
- **Wi-Fi:** These phones are the cheapest part of the chain. A weak router drops connections more than a weak phone does. Marks are held on the phone and sent again after a drop.

## Battery life

These are estimates, not measured results. The phones have not been tested with BIS.

- **Estimate:** about 10 to 14 hours with the screen on and Wi-Fi connected, running a page as light as BIS. The Redmi A5 and POCO C71 (5,200mAh) should be about the same. The TECNO Spark Go 2 (5,000mAh) a little less.

### How to measure it

1. Charge one Redmi A5 to 100%.
2. Open BIS on it with the screen on, at the brightness used at the venue.
3. Leave it for a few hours on a sample round and note the percentage drop.
4. Multiply that out for the full day.
