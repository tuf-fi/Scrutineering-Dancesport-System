Start here

What every signed-in person has

These work the same for everyone.

Pop-up messages

Short messages with a small sound appear on the screen when something needs you. Staff see them for new entry requests, new account links and membership payments. The admin hears the same ones as the secretariat. Athletes, coaches and teams see what the secretariat decided, and the same message is kept under Notifications. Adjudicators and the compere get none.

Required fields and saving

A field that must be filled in has a red * after its label. Buttons that save are switched off while the save is under way, so pressing twice never saves twice. On a phone the fields do not zoom in when you tap them.

Your password

Everyone who signs in with an email can change their own password. Athletes, coaches and teams can also Sign out everywhere. Staff can turn on two-step sign-in: after the password you type a 6-digit code from an app on your phone.

Phone and laptop

The same pages fit any screen. On a phone, the menu is the three lines at the top right, and athlete, coach and team pages have a strip of links under the top bar.

Safe by design

Every action is checked against your role. A role that is not allowed to do something sees "Your account cannot do this."

05

Adjudicators

You mark on your own phone. Nothing you mark can be lost.

Sign in · Waiting · Mark · Review · Sign · Submitted · Final placing

Sign in

The website address followed by /adjudicator

Type your 6-digit PIN, or scan the code on your PIN card with your phone's camera.

  • Your PIN works only around the competition date, on one phone at a time.
  • Signing in on another phone signs this one out.

Waiting

Appears after you sign in

Wait for the next round.

  • The round appears by itself when the scrutineer starts it. Keep the screen on and the phone charged.

Mark: early rounds

Appears when a round starts

Tap the numbers of the dancers you want to go through to the next round.

  • Choose exactly the number shown at the top ("Mark an X for exactly 12").
  • The counter at the bottom shows how many you have marked.
  • Dancers are grouped by heat. Tap again to remove a mark.

Review and sign

Press Submit

Check your choices, then sign.

  • Review your selection: check the numbers. They cannot be changed once sent.
  • Press Final and submit, sign with your finger (Clear to sign again) and press Finalize.
  • Your signature is printed on the scores sheet.

Submitted

Appears after Finalize

Your marks are with the scrutineer.

  • The screen changes by itself when the next round starts.

Mark: the final

Appears when a final starts

Place every finalist, 1st to last, in each dance.

  • Tap a number to place it in the next free place.
  • Use the arrows to move a placing up or down, or the cross to take it out.
  • The counter shows how many are placed. Press Submit when everyone is placed, then sign as before.

If something goes wrong

Your marks are saved on the phone first.

  • Connection drops: carry on and press Finalize. The phone sends your marks when it can, and the scrutineer can see it holds them.
  • Page refreshed or browser closed: open the address again. Your marks come back.
  • Phone dies: use your paper sheet and give it to the scrutineer, who types it in.
  • Pressed Finalize by mistake: tell the scrutineer before they approve the round. Marks cannot be changed from the phone.
  • PIN refused: ask the admin for a new one.

Scrutineer

You run every round. Use a laptop and keep it open all day.

Rounds · Heats · Live board · Paper marks · Recall · Results · Printouts · Security

Rounds

Menu: Rounds

See where the competition is and every round still to run.

  • Plan rounds: press once so every event with entries gets its rounds.
  • Start: puts the round on the floor and on the adjudicators' phones.
  • A round will not start while entries are unpaid. The message tells you how many.
  • Take off the floor: use it for a wrong round. Possible until an adjudicator has submitted.
  • The black box shows the round on the floor, how many adjudicators have submitted, and shortcuts to Live board, Heats, Recall and Results.

Heats

Menu: Heats

Split a big round into groups that dance together.

  • Choose the round and how many dancers per heat.
  • Split evenly shares them out. Use the heat box on a row to move one dancer.
  • Press Save heats.

Live board

Menu: Live board

Watch each adjudicator's phone.

  • Each adjudicator goes from Waiting to Submitted.
  • A phone silent for 20 seconds is flagged. "Marks saved on the phone, not sent yet" means the phone has them and is waiting for a connection.
  • When everyone is in, press Review the recall (or Review the results).

Paper marks

Menu: Paper marks

Type in an adjudicator's paper sheet when their phone could not send.

  • Choose the round and the adjudicator. Only adjudicators who have not submitted are listed.
  • Tap exactly what is on the sheet: an X for each couple, or every finalist in order in a final.
  • Press Submit, check the numbers against the sheet and press Enter marks.
  • They count like marks from the phone, and the adjudicator shows as submitted on the Live board.
  • A sheet cannot be changed once entered. Only while a round is being marked or reviewed. The chairperson does not have this page.

Recall

Menu: Recall

See who goes through to the next round.

  • X marks per dancer and the cut-off for the next round.
  • Tie at the cut-off: choose Recall all those tied, or Recall only those above, and press Propose decision.
  • The chairperson then confirms. The round cannot be approved until they do.

Results

Menu: Results

Each adjudicator's marks and how the round works out.

  • See every adjudicator's marks, the X total and the places. In a final you also see the skating tables.
  • Tie in a final: propose keeping the shared place, or a tie-break order. The chairperson confirms.
  • Approve results: locks the marks, saves the result, adds the ranking points and takes a backup.
  • Publish results: makes the result public and shows it to the compere.
  • Print summary prints the round.

Printouts

Print summary, or All printouts

Paper sheets for any event or round.

  • Heat list, list of dancers going through, scores sheet, final results and blank mark sheets.
  • Press Print on the sheet, or Back to return.

Scores sheet and blank sheets

All printouts

Marks of every adjudicator on paper, and empty sheets for a backup.

Security

Menu: Security

Turn on two-step sign-in and change your password.

  • Backup warning: a red note at the top of the screen means a backup folder failed. Tell the admin. Results are still saved.
  • You cannot confirm your own tie proposal. The chairperson does that.

The whole competition, step by step

How it works

The big picture

Everyone has a part. Information moves from one person to the next, and ends on the website.

AdminSets up the competition, staff, adjudicators, fees and points
SecretariatEnters dancers, gives back numbers, plans the program
ScrutineerStarts each round
AdjudicatorsMark on their phones
ScrutineerChecks the marks and settles ties with the chairperson
ScrutineerApproves and publishes the result
Compere and visitorsThe compere announces it. Everyone sees it on the website

How marks become results

Early rounds (round 1, round 2, quarter-final, semi-final). Each adjudicator marks an X for the number of couples to go on (the recall number). BOSS counts the X marks for each couple. The couples with the most X marks go through. If couples are tied right at the cut-off, the scrutineer proposes whether to take all those tied or only those above, and the chairperson confirms.

The final. Each adjudicator places every finalist, 1st to last, in each dance. BOSS works out the places with the official Skating System: a couple wins a place when a majority of the adjudicators (more than half) place them at that place or better. If couples still cannot be separated, the scrutineer proposes keeping the shared place or a tie-break dance, and the chairperson confirms.

Ranking points. When the scrutineer approves a result, points from the admin's points table are added to each dancer's ranking. Rankings are kept per category, for example Junior I E1 Latin.

Words used

Back numberThe number pinned to a dancer's back.
HeatA group dancing at the same time when a round is too big for the floor.
RecallBeing chosen to go to the next round.
X markAn adjudicator's vote to recall a dancer.
PlacingAn adjudicator's ranking in the final: 1 is best.
PanelThe adjudicators who mark an event.

Admin

Create the competition

Open Competitions and press New competition. Add the name, date, venue, organizer and a card photo. Set the entries and fees deadlines if you have them.

6

Secretariat

Add the events

Open Events and add each event with its age group, class, style and dances. Set the Kind (Beginners, Ranking, Inter-school or Closed to a province).

7

Secretariat

Add the teams and coaches

Open Teams and Coaches and add each one once, as named on the entry forms.

Part 2 · Take entries

8

Athletes, coaches, teams

Make accounts and link them

Each person presses Sign in on the website and creates an account. Coaches and teams then send a link request. The secretariat approves them under Account links.

9

Secretariat

Enter dancers from entry forms

Open Competitors. Use Add competitor or Import CSV, then press the first icon on a row to enter the dancer in events and record how they paid.

10

Secretariat

Approve entries sent online

Open Entry requests. Check each payment screenshot against the money received. Press the tick to approve, or the cross to turn down with a reason.

11

Secretariat

Confirm memberships

Open Athletes and confirm each membership payment once the money arrives.

12

Secretariat

Give out back numbers

On Competitors, press Assign back numbers once the entries are in.

13

Admin

Choose the panels

On Adjudicators and panels, open the Panels tab and choose which adjudicators mark each event.

14

Secretariat

Plan the day

Open Schedule to set the order of the events. Then open Program, press Generate, set the start time and add breaks. The program is now on the website for everyone.

Part 3 · The day before

15

Admin

Print the PIN cards

On Adjudicators and panels, choose the competition, tick the adjudicators and press Print PIN cards. Hand them out privately.

16

Admin

Check the system

Make sure there is no backup warning at the top of the screen and that the server clock is right. PINs work only around the competition date.

17

Secretariat

Print the heat lists

Open an event and press Heat list, or open All printouts.

18

Everyone

Sign in and test

Staff sign in on their laptops. Each adjudicator signs in with their PIN and sees Waiting for the next round.

Part 4 · On the day, round by round

19

Scrutineer

Plan the rounds (once)

Open Rounds and press Plan rounds. Every event with entries gets its rounds.

20

Scrutineer

Split into heats if needed

If a round is too big for the floor, open Heats, press Split evenly and Save heats.

21

Scrutineer

Start the round

On Rounds, press Start on the round. It appears on the adjudicators' phones and the compere's screen shows the numbers to call. If it will not start, some entries are unpaid: the secretariat records the payment or scratches the dancer.

22

Adjudicators

Mark and sign

Early rounds: tap the numbers of the dancers to go through, exactly the number shown. Final: place every finalist 1st to last. Press Submit, check, then Final and submit, sign and press Finalize.

23

Scrutineer

Watch the live board

Watch each phone go from Waiting to Submitted. If a phone cannot send, take the paper sheet and type it in on Paper marks.

24

Scrutineer and chairperson

Settle any tie

Open Recall. If couples are tied at the cut-off, the scrutineer chooses an option and presses Propose decision. The chairperson opens Recall and presses Confirm tie decision.

25

Scrutineer

Approve and publish

Open Results. Check the table, press Approve results, then Publish results. The result is now on the website and on the compere's screen, and the ranking points are added.

26

Compere

Announce

The compere reads the numbers that go through (Recalled), or the places from last to first (Results). Press the arrows or number keys for the next announcement.

27

Scrutineer

Next round

Go back to step 21 for the next round: round 1, round 2, quarter-final, semi-final and finally the final. In the final the adjudicators place every finalist, and a tie in the final is settled the same way as step 24.

28

Secretariat

Late changes

A dancer withdraws: open the event and press Scratch. A dancer arrives late: add them under Late entries. They cannot dance until they have paid.

Part 5 · After the competition

29

Everyone

Results and rankings are public

Visitors read the results on the competition page and the new rankings on Rankings. Athletes, coaches and teams see their results on their own pages.

30

Scrutineer

Print the results

Use Print summary on Results, or All printouts for score sheets and final results.

31

Admin

Check the money and the backups

Open Payments to see what is paid and what is still owed. Check there is no backup warning.

Help

Common questions

Quick answers. If these do not help, ask the secretariat or the admin.

QuestionAnswer
I forgot my password.Athletes, coaches and teams: ask the secretariat for a new one. Staff: ask the admin.
My entry or payment was turned down.Open Notifications or My requests to read the reason, fix it and send it again.
I cannot see my dancers.Your account must be linked first. Open Link your team (or coach) and send the request.
A round will not start.Some entries are not paid. The secretariat records the payment or scratches the dancer.
Results are not on the website.The scrutineer has not published them yet.
A phone says marks are not sent.They are saved. Move to mobile data or other Wi-Fi, or use the paper sheet.
Two couples have the same place in a final.The scrutineer proposes how to settle it, the chairperson confirms, then the round can be approved.
An adjudicator's PIN is refused.The admin makes a new one on Adjudicators and panels. A PIN only works around the competition date.
A wrong round was started.The scrutineer presses Take off the floor. Possible until an adjudicator has submitted.
