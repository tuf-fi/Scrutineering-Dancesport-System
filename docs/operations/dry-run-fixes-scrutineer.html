BOSS · 20 November 2026 · Changes to the scrutineer pages

Dry-run fixes: Scrutineer

Three notes from the scrutineer: certificates you can edit, full control of heats, and results that seemed to disappear after approving event 1.

Fixes against the dry-run notes

NoteFix
Certificates should be editable: font size, style, landscape or portraitNew Certificates page for the scrutineer and the admin. One layout for every competition: page orientation, a background picture, and for each line its wording, font, size, bold, italic and capitals. A layout can be exported to a file and imported again.
Heats: editable beyond the given formatThe scrutineer could already edit heats before approval. Added Merge heats, Swap couples and Move a heat next to Split evenly and the per-couple heat dropdown (its last option makes a new heat). Editing continues after the round has started.
After approving event 1, its result data seemed gone; other events were fineThe data was not lost. The Results page followed the round on the floor, and approving takes the round off the floor, so the page jumped to another round. The page now keeps the round you are looking at.

Heats during a round

Heats can still be saved while a round is being marked or in review. Adjudicators' marks stay as they are. Adjudicator phones and the compere read heats live, so saving a change to a started round now asks first, then tells those screens to refresh at once. Heats cannot change after the round is approved, or while a redance waits for its marks.

Certificates

Words in braces are filled in for each finalist: {organizer} {competition} {name} {team} {place} {event} {grade} {number} {venue} {date}. A line left empty, or whose words are all blank (a team line for a couple with no team), is not printed. Fonts are Sans serif, Serif, Script and Monospace, so they print the same offline. The background is a JPEG, PNG or WebP picture, shrunk to fit. The built-in layout is the one used before.

How to test

1. Results stay put after approving

  1. Sign in as scrutineer. Open Results from the menu (no round in the address).
  2. Choose event 1's final while it is in review. Press Approve results, then Approve.
  3. Expected: the page still shows event 1 with status Approved and its table. It does not switch to another event, and Publish results now appears for event 1.
  4. The program shows event 1 as Completed. The compere shows its results once published.

2. Heats

  1. Open Heats and choose a round with at least 2 heats.
  2. Swap couples: pick two couples, Apply. Their heat numbers trade. Nothing is saved yet.
  3. Merge heats: merge heat 3 into heat 1. The heat count in the dark panel drops.
  4. Move a heat: move heat 3 to position 1. The other heats shift.
  5. In the table, set a couple's heat to the last number (new heat) to create a heat.
  6. Press Save heats. On a round being marked, a box asks Change heats during the round?. Confirm.
  7. Expected: “Heats saved”. On an adjudicator's phone for that round the heat groups update without reloading, and the compere call list follows the new heats.
  8. After the round is approved the heat controls are hidden. While a redance waits for marks they are hidden too.

3. Certificates

  1. Sign in as scrutineer and open Certificates (admin: Certificates under System). The chairperson and secretariat do not see it.
  2. Set Page to Portrait. Pick the line Name, set Font to Serif, Size to 28 and turn on Italic. The preview on the right changes as you go.
  3. Edit the wording of Opening words. Clear the Team wording to hide that line.
  4. Choose a Background picture, then Save layout. Expected: “Certificate layout saved”.
  5. Export layout, press Reset (back to the built-in look), then Import layout with the file and Save. The layout returns.
  6. Open an approved final's Print certificates. Expected: one page per finalist in the saved orientation, with the saved fonts, wording and background. In the print dialog the paper is A4 in the same orientation.
