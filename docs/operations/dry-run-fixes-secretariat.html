Dry-run fixes: Secretariat

Three notes from the secretariat: no Beginners kind when adding an event, full control of names and birth years, and a paid or not-paid pill with a way to update it.

Fixes against the dry-run notes

NoteFix
Add event: remove BeginnerThe Kind list in Add event no longer offers Beginners (BGB). Editing an event that already has that kind still shows it, so nothing existing breaks.
The secretariat should edit an athlete's birth year and name, and athlete, coach and team details. Earlier records must stay linked to the edited nameThe secretariat can now correct an athlete's name and date of birth, and a coach's name, on the Athletes, Coaches and Teams pages. Before, only an admin could, and the secretariat got an error. A new name is copied to the athlete's account, athlete record, every entry and any entry requests, so entries, results and rankings stay attached.
Payment details: show an icon or pill for paid or not paid, and let the secretariat update itMembership on the Athletes page is now a Paid or Not paid pill. Every athlete row has a check (mark paid) or X (mark not paid) button, including athletes with no account, since some events need a membership fee. The athlete's Entries panel shows each entry's payment status with Mark paid and Mark unpaid. Entries with no payment (none required) show nothing.
Group actionEach athlete row has a checkbox, with a select-all in the header. With rows ticked, the toolbar offers Mark N paid and Mark N not paid.
Smaller touch-upsAthletes known only from an entry form now have a pencil to edit their entry. The Which entry? box was restyled: one rounded list, team name under each entry, gold back number, and an arrow that turns gold on hover. Delete entry is now the yellow button.

Names and birth years

Entries, results and marks already belong to the competitor record, so a rename never loses them. What used to break was the link between an athlete's account and their entries, which is matched by name. Correcting a name or birth date now updates every place that holds it in one step: the account, the athlete record, each entry (including the same person in a solo and a couple entry), and entry requests. Each correction is written to the audit log with the old and new values. Athletes can still ask for a change from their own page; the secretariat approves or turns it down on Profile changes, and an approval updates every record too.

Payments

A payment belongs to the team and covers several entries, so marking one entry paid or unpaid changes all the entries on that payment. Marking a payment unpaid does not take back the membership year it recorded; membership has its own Paid or Not paid button. A membership marked paid by hand is recorded as Cash.

Membership belongs to the person, not to the account. Marking it paid or unpaid is matched by name and updates the athlete's account, athlete record and every entry together, so a couple entry and a solo entry of the same athlete agree. One catch: a couple entry keeps a single membership year for the pair, so marking one partner paid marks the other on that entry too.

How to test

1. Add event

  1. Sign in as secretariat. Open Events and press Add event.
  2. Expected: the Kind list has no Beginners (BGB). Edit an existing Beginners event: its kind is still shown.

2. Correct a name and birth year

  1. Open Athletes. On an athlete with an account, press the pencil (Correct name or date of birth).
  2. Change the name and the date of birth, then Save. Expected: “Corrected”, with no permission error.
  3. Open the athlete's details. Expected: the entries, results and rankings are still listed under the new name, and the Athletes page still shows one row.
  4. Also edit an athlete with no account, a competitor entry and a coach. Names and birth dates save for all of them.

3. Paid or not paid

  1. On Athletes, find the Membership pill. In the row's icons press the check (Mark membership as paid). Expected: the pill turns Paid. Try it on an athlete with an account and on one known only from an entry.
  2. Press the X (Mark membership as unpaid). The pill returns to Not paid.
  3. Group action: tick the checkbox on three rows, or the box in the header for all of them. Press Mark 3 paid. Expected: all three pills turn Paid. Mark 3 not paid reverses it.
  4. Open an athlete who has entries and go to Entries. Each paid entry shows a pill. Press Mark unpaid: the pill changes to Unpaid and the button becomes Mark paid. Press it to record the payment again.
  5. An athlete whose entries need no payment shows no pill in that list.
