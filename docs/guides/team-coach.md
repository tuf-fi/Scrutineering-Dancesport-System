# Team and coach guide

A team or a coach makes their own account to follow their dancers and, for a team, to send entries online. The secretariat still keeps the records.

## Making your account
1. Press **Sign in** at the top of the public site (on a phone, open the menu first), choose **Team** or **Coach** and press **Continue**, then **Create an account**.
2. Enter the name, an email and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. Forgot your password? Ask the secretariat; they give you a new one.

## Link your team (or coach)
Your account sees nothing until it is linked to the team or coach record the secretariat keeps. Open **Link your team** (or **Link your coach**), pick your record and send the request. The secretariat approves it under **Account links**. Every page that needs the link tells you while it is missing, and you can take a waiting request back.

## Your pages
- **Account**: your details, picture and what you are linked to. **Edit account** changes them.
- **Dancers**: the dancers on your record.
- **Results** and **Rankings**: how those dancers placed, and where they stand.
- **Entries**: the events they are entered in.
- **Payments** (team): what has been paid and how.
- **Notifications**: what the secretariat decided. A new one arrives as a pop-up with a short sound; press it to open the page it is about.
- **Password**: change it here.

## Sending entries online (team)
1. Open **Enter events**, pick a dancer and the competition, and tick the events.
2. The fee and any warning show before you send. Pay to GCash, Maya or the bank account shown and add a screenshot of the payment.
3. Press **Send entry**. It appears under **My requests** as waiting. The secretariat approves it, which enters the dancers and records the payment, or turns it down with a reason so you can fix it and send it again. You can take a waiting request back.
4. You can only enter dancers on your own team.
