# Compere guide

Your screen is a laptop or a projector, usually the LED wall. Open the address, sign in as the compere and press **F** for full screen. The controls at the top fade out after a few seconds so the wall shows only the announcement; move the mouse to bring them back.

## Moving around
- **Space, Enter or the right arrow** goes to the next announcement. **Backspace or the left arrow** goes back. **Home** and **End** jump to the first and last.
- **Up next** at the top right tells you what the next screen is before you press.
- Six views, keys **1** to **6**. A view is dimmed until it has something to show.

## The views
1. **Next event.** The event going to the floor, with the **next event** in the running order above it so the next couples can get ready. On the right: the heat and how many each adjudicator selects (or Final). Below: the back numbers. For a semi-final or a final each couple's name and team are shown too.
2. **Recalled.** The numbers that go through to the next round (with names for a semi-final or final).
3. **Finalists.** The couples in the final, with number, name and team.
4. **Results.** A "Results" slide, then each place from last up to 1st. The top three stand as bars; the other places list on the right. It shows only the event on screen.
5. **Program.** What is on the floor, what is up next, and the running order around them. A dot shows each line: red finished, green on the floor, blue up next, grey not started. A round counts as on the floor only once marking has started.
6. **Live.** How many adjudicators have sent their marks for each round on the floor. Several rounds at once sit side by side. It never shows the marks.

## Results held until the awarding
If the competition holds its results, nothing about the final placings appears (no Finalists, no Results) until the scrutineer or chairperson presses **Reveal results**. Then the Results slides for every event appear in event order, ready to read.

## If something looks wrong
- **"Waiting for the next announcement":** the scrutineer has not published a round yet.
- **A final shows nothing:** it has not been published, or its results are held until the awarding. Ask the scrutineer.
- **"Couldn't load this":** the connection failed. Press **Try again**.

The compere cannot change anything about the competition.
