# Scrutineer guide

You run the rounds from your laptop. Keep its window open all day.

## Before the first round
- `https://<the site address>/api/health` says ok. No warning about a backup folder at the top of the screen.
- **Rounds**: press **Plan rounds** once. Every event gets its rounds.
- Every adjudicator is signed in on a phone.

## Each round
1. **Rounds**: choose the round, press **Start round**. It appears on the phones. If every entry in the event is not paid, it will not start; the message says how many.
2. **Live board**: watch each phone go from waiting to submitted. A phone quiet for 20 seconds is flagged. "Marks saved on the phone, not sent yet" means it has them and is waiting for the connection.
3. **Recall** (early rounds) or **Results** (final): check the table. A tie at the cut-off: pick how to settle it, press **Propose decision**, and the chairperson presses **Confirm tie decision**.
4. **Tie in the final:** if the marks cannot separate two couples, a **Tie in the final** section appears on **Results** and approving is blocked. Choose to keep the shared place, or settle it with a tie-break (the couples dance once more; type each couple's place from the sheet), press **Propose decision**, and the chairperson confirms.
5. **Approve**, then **Publish results**. Approving saves the result, the ranking points and a backup. Publishing makes it public and shows it to the compere.

## Problems
- **Wrong round started:** **Take off the floor**, possible until a phone has submitted.
- **A phone cannot send:** the marks are safe on the phone and go out by themselves when it has a connection again. Ask the adjudicator to switch to mobile data or another network, and watch the Live board.
- **This laptop stops or loses internet:** sign in again on this or any other laptop. Nothing is stored on the laptop, and nothing saved is lost.
- **Backup warning:** a backup folder failed. Tell the admin; results are still saved on the server.

## Typing in a paper sheet
When an adjudicator's phone cannot send their marks, they hand in their paper sheet.
1. Open **Paper marks**. The round on the floor is chosen; pick another from the list if you need to. It works only while the round is being marked or reviewed.
2. Choose the adjudicator. Only the ones who have not submitted are listed.
3. Type in exactly what is on the sheet: an X for each couple marked (the recall number), or every finalist in order in a final, dance by dance.
4. Press **Submit**, check the numbers against the sheet, and press **Enter marks**. They count the same as marks sent from the phone, and the adjudicator shows as submitted on the **Live board**.

A sheet cannot be changed once entered. If the adjudicator's phone later sends different marks, the server refuses them: the sheet you entered stands. The chairperson cannot use this page.
