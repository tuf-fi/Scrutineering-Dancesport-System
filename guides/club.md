# Club guide

1. **Register your club:** open the club sign-in page and choose to register. Read the privacy notice and tick the box.
2. **Dancers:** add each dancer or couple with the age group. Only a name and age group are needed.
3. **Events:** choose a dancer, tick the events, see the total, press **Save entries**.
4. **Payments:** pay the total by GCash, Maya or bank transfer to the account shown. Then press **Go to payment**, enter the amount and reference number, add a screenshot of the receipt, and **Send for checking**.
5. The organizer checks it. **Approved** means your entries are confirmed. **Rejected** shows why; fix it and send again.
6. **Account:** change your password, and connect Messenger to be told when a payment is decided.

A paid entry cannot be withdrawn here. Ask the secretariat.
