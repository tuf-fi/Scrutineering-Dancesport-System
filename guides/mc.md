# MC guide

Your screen is on Laptop 3 or a projector. Open the address and sign in as the MC.

- The screen shows the announcements for the round just published: places, in order, in large type.
- **Next announcement** and **Previous announcement** move through them. Nothing is public until the scrutineer publishes the round, so there is nothing to announce before then.
- If the screen shows nothing, ask the scrutineer whether the round has been published.
