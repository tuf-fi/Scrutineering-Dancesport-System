# Adjudicator guide

You mark on your own phone. Nothing you do can be lost: your marks are saved on the phone first.

## Before the first round
1. Connect to the internet (mobile data or any Wi-Fi). Open the address the scrutineer gives you.
2. Type your six-digit PIN, or scan the QR code on your PIN card with your phone's camera. It works only on the day of the competition, give or take a day, and on one phone at a time: signing in on another phone signs this one out. You see **Waiting for the next round**.
3. Keep the screen on and the phone charged. Do not sign out.

## Marking
- The round appears by itself when the scrutineer starts it.
- **Early rounds (recall):** mark an X for exactly the number of couples shown. The counter says how many are marked and warns if you have too many.
- **Final:** place every finalist, first to last, in each dance. The counter says how many are placed.
- Press **Submit** and check the summary. Sign in the box with your finger (press **Clear** to sign again), then **Confirm**. You see **Submitted**. Your signature is printed on the scores sheet.

## If something goes wrong
- **Connection drops:** carry on. Press Confirm anyway; the phone says it is saved and sends the marks when it can. The scrutineer can see it is waiting.
- **Page refreshed or browser closed:** open the address again. What you marked comes back.
- **Phone dies:** use your paper sheet. Give it to the scrutineer, who types it in.
- **You pressed Confirm by mistake:** tell the scrutineer before they approve the round.

Marks cannot be changed from the phone after Confirm.
