# Secretariat guide

You look after entries and the order of the day, on your laptop.

## Before the event
1. **Competitors**: check names and clubs. **Import CSV** adds a list; **Add competitor** adds one.
2. Assign back numbers: **Assign back numbers** gives every competitor one. Change a single one with its back-number button.
3. **Events**: check each event's entries. **Schedule**: put the events in running order.

## On the day
- **Late entries**: add a dancer to an event. Choose whether they paid cash at the venue; a late entry that has not paid cannot dance.
- **Scratch** a dancer who withdraws from an event. **Restore** undoes it before the event starts.
- From an event page, print the **Heat list** and **Recall list**.

Fees for late entries come from the fees the organizer set online; if late entries are refused, the fees have not been set.
