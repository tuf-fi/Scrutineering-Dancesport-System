# Secretariat guide

You look after entries and the order of the day, on Laptop 1.

## Before the event (with internet)
1. **Cloud sync**: download the competition. Only paid entries come down; the screen says how many were left out and why.
2. **Competitors**: check names and clubs. **Import CSV** adds a list; **Add competitor** adds one.
3. Assign back numbers: **Assign back numbers** gives every competitor one. Change a single one with its back-number button.
4. **Events**: check each event's entries. **Schedule**: put the events in running order.

## On the day
- **Late entries**: add a dancer to an event. Choose whether they paid cash at the venue; a late entry that has not paid cannot dance.
- **Scratch** a dancer who withdraws from an event. **Restore** undoes it before the event starts.
- From an event page, print the **Heat list** and **Recall list**.

## After
- **Cloud sync** should say nothing is waiting. Ask an admin to release the competition.

Fees for late entries come from the fees the organizer set online; if late entries are refused, the fees have not been set.
