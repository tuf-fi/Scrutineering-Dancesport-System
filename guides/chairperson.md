# Chairperson guide

You oversee the scoring. You see the scrutineer's screens but cannot enter marks.

## Your part
- **Ties at the recall cut-off:** when several couples have the same number of X marks across the recall number, the scrutineer proposes how to settle it on the **Recall** screen (**Propose decision**). When it says "Proposed. Waiting for the chairperson to confirm.", read the proposal and press **Confirm tie decision**. The round cannot be approved until you do.
- **Ties in a final:** if two couples still share a place, **Results** shows **Tie in the final**. The scrutineer proposes keeping the shared place or a tie-break order; check it against the tie-break sheet, then press **Confirm tie decision**. The final cannot be approved until you do.
- **Results:** before the scrutineer publishes a final, look at **Results**: the places and the skating tables should make sense to you.
- **Live board:** shows who has submitted and who has not.

## You cannot
- Start, approve or publish a round, or enter paper marks. Ask the scrutineer.

Every decision is recorded with your name and the time.
