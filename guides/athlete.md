# Athlete guide

You make your own account to keep your details and pay your membership. The secretariat enters you in events.

## Making your account
1. Open **Athlete sign-in** at the top of the public site and press **Create an account**.
2. Enter your name, your email and a password of at least 8 characters. Use the same email as on your entry form, so the secretariat does not charge your membership twice.
3. Forgot your password? Ask the secretariat; they give you a new one.

## Your page
- **Your details**: date of birth, contact number, province, address, team and coach. Only the secretariat sees these.
- **Membership**: pay the fee shown to one of the ways to pay listed, then enter how you paid and the reference number and press **Send payment**. It shows **Waiting for the secretariat** until they confirm it. If they cannot confirm it you see why, and you can send it again. A paid membership lasts until December.
